# Release 0.8.15 (16 October 2026)

What was published as 0.8.15 and what was checked before and after. 0.8.15 relocates the
readers by content when an update moves their code, and certifies with `code-208704-r2`.

## Published

- Release: https://github.com/fodaveg/tyrian-companion-nexus/releases/tag/0.8.15
- Tag `0.8.15` (annotated) on `ccb02fa`.
- Assets, both `uploaded`:
  - `tyrian_companion_nexus.dll`, 4689920 bytes, sha256
    `fe3141c0c0d9fbaaa038dd35cb3e9fcb8da00683b75379d0341dcb4ea3f63704`;
  - `tyrian_companion_nexus.dll.sha256`, in `sha256sum` format.
- The DLL downloaded back from the release hashes to the same sha256.
- It needs the plugin 0.7.6 or later. The protocol with the plugin does not change.
